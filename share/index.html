Four steps, and the order matters.

Post first. Claim last. We can only check a post that is already up.

1 · Post your week

Open Tessera 168, tap Share on the board, and you get a picture of your week — every hour in its own colour, with no name, no avatar and no dates unless you switch them on. Post it anywhere, and tag @tessera168 so we can find it.

2 · Get 100 likes

Any public post on Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, YouTube or Reddit counts. Likes, hearts, upvotes — whatever that platform calls the number, a hundred of them is the bar.

3 · Send the proof

Email ioniclabs.support@gmail.com with a link to the post and a screenshot showing the like count. The post has to be public and still up when we look.

4 · Get your year

We send back an App Store offer code for a free year of Tessera 168 Pro. Redeem it in the app — Pro ▸ Redeem an offer code — or in the App Store. Nothing is charged when the year ends unless you decide to continue.

The small print, unfolded.

Everything you could feel cheated by later is one of these lines.

How many times can I claim?

Once. One free year of Pro per person, whichever platform the post is on and however many posts you make.

How is the code sent?

By email, to the address the proof came from, within a week. It is an App Store offer code — we never see a card number and you never enter one.

What counts as a valid post?

A public post about Tessera 168 that tags @tessera168, with at least 100 likes, still public when we check it. A post deleted or made private before we look cannot be verified, so it cannot be paid.

I already bought Pro. Is this useless to me?

No — give the code to a friend. It is a code, not an unlock tied to your account.

What happens when the free year ends?

Pro's extras switch off and the app keeps working: the whole 168-hour board, the built-in purposes and written notes stay free forever. Nothing renews on its own and nothing is charged without you buying it.

Can you change or end this offer?

Yes, at any time, and this page will say so. Codes already sent stay valid for their full year.

Do you keep my email or my post?

We keep the claim email until the code is sent and the year is over, so the same person is not paid twice. Nothing else. See the privacy policy.

Written to a person, answered by a person.

Claims, partnerships, or a question about the offer — write to ioniclabs.support@gmail.com and you will hear back from the person who made the app.
