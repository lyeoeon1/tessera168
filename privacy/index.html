Privacy Policy — Tessera 168

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Tiếng Việt ↓

Tessera 168 is made by Tống Hưng. This policy covers the iOS app com.tonghung.app168 and nothing else.

The short version

Tessera 168 does not collect anything. There is no account, no sign-in, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Your week is a single file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app knows lives in one JSON file in the app's Application Support directory on your device:

  • which purpose you gave each of the 168 hours in a week, and for how many weeks back
  • notes you typed, or dictated, on an hour
  • side tasks you wrote inside an hour
  • your settings: theme, language, sound, motion, haptics, app icon, notifications

That file is encrypted at rest by iOS (protected until the first unlock after your phone starts) and is included in your device's own iCloud or iTunes backup, in the same way every other app's data is. We cannot read that backup. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Export and Delete all data both live in Settings › Privacy inside the app. Export hands the file to the iOS share sheet so you can keep a copy. Delete removes every hour, every note and every setting, permanently, on the spot. There is nothing on our side to also delete, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

Microphone and Speech Recognition — only if you use a voice note. Speech is transcribed on your device: the app requires Apple's on-device recogniser and refuses a language whose on-device model has not been downloaded, rather than sending the audio anywhere. No audio is written to disk and none is kept after the sentence is transcribed. Only the resulting text is saved, as the hour's note.

Notifications — only if you turn on an hour's alarm or the unfinished-hours nudge. The app asks the first time you turn one of these on, never at launch. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device; there is no push server.

The app does not ask for and cannot access: contacts, photos, camera, location, health data, calendars, or motion data.

Purchases

Tessera Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card, your name, your email or your Apple Account. The app asks StoreKit one question — whether this Apple Account is entitled to Pro — and that answer stays on the device. Purchases are covered by Apple's privacy policy and the standard End User Licence Agreement.

Subscriptions are managed in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or from the Pro tab inside the app, which opens that same screen. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only outbound traffic is Apple's: the App Store, when the paywall loads prices or verifies a purchase. Every link in the app that opens a web page is listed in Sources/Model/Lang.swift and there are three of them.

Children

The app is not directed at children, collects nothing from anyone, and has no user-generated content that anybody else can see.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because nothing is collected, no change can retroactively affect data we hold — there is none.

Micro và Nhận dạng giọng nói — chỉ khi bạn dùng ghi chú giọng nói. Thông báo — chỉ khi bạn bật chuông cho một giờ, hoặc lời nhắc về những giờ chưa rõ. App hỏi vào lần đầu bạn bật một trong số đó, không bao giờ hỏi lúc khởi động. Mọi thông báo đều được lên lịch ngay trên máy bạn; không có máy chủ đẩy thông báo nào.

App không xin và không truy cập được: danh bạ, ảnh, camera, vị trí, dữ liệu sức khoẻ, lịch, hay dữ liệu chuyển động.
