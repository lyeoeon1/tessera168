Privacy policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tessera 168 iOS application (bundle identifier com.tonghung.app168, “the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies only to the App and to this support website. It does not apply to Apple's services, which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. Summary

  • The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no server operated by us, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device, under your control. You can export it or erase it at any time from inside the App.
  • The only network traffic is between your device and Apple, for App Store purchases.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or have access to any of the following: your name, email address, phone number, Apple Account, payment details, device identifiers, IP address, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, health data, usage statistics, crash reports, or the content you create in the App.

The App contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting frameworks. Its privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares no tracking, no tracking domains and no collected data types, and the App Store product page shows “Data Not Collected”.

4. Information stored on your device

To function, the App keeps a single data file in its private container on your iPhone. It contains:

  • the purpose you assigned to each hour of each week you have recorded;
  • notes you typed or dictated for an hour;
  • side tasks you wrote inside an hour;
  • your preferences: theme, language, sound, motion, haptics, app icon and notification settings;
  • a local record of whether Tessera Pro is unlocked on this device.

This file never leaves your device through the App. iOS protects it with file-level encryption and includes it in your own iCloud or computer backup under the same rules as every other app's data; we cannot read those backups.

5. Permissions

The App requests a system permission only at the moment a feature needs it, never at launch. Every feature below is optional, and declining a permission does not affect the rest of the App.

PermissionWhen it is requestedHow it is used
Microphone and Speech Recognition When you start a voice note Speech is transcribed on your device using Apple's on-device recogniser. The App requires on-device recognition and refuses to record in a language whose on-device model is not installed, rather than send audio to a server. No audio is saved; only the resulting text is kept as the hour's note.
Notifications When you turn on an alarm for an hour or the reminder for unfinished hours Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server and no remote notification.

The App does not request access to contacts, calendars, photos, camera, location, health, motion, Bluetooth or the local network.

6. In-App Purchases

Tessera Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription and as a one-time purchase, both sold exclusively through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details, name, email or Apple Account. The App queries StoreKit only to learn whether the current Apple Account is entitled to Pro, and that answer is stored locally.

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Purchases are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and this App's Terms of Use.

7. Third-party services

The App integrates no third-party services. The only external party involved in its operation is Apple, through iOS system frameworks (StoreKit, UserNotifications, Speech) that run on your device. Links inside the App open only this policy, the support page, and the Terms of Use.

8. Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored by us, there is no server-side data to secure. On the device, the App relies on iOS data protection: its container is encrypted at rest and is inaccessible to other apps.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep it. Inside the App, Settings › Privacy offers:

  • Export — hands your complete data file to the iOS share sheet so you can keep or move a copy;
  • Delete all data — permanently erases every hour, note, task and setting from the device immediately.

Deleting the App from your device also removes its container. There is nothing for us to delete on request, because we hold nothing.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It contains no user-generated content visible to other people, no chat and no social features.

11. Your rights

Under privacy laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not collect or process any personal data, there is nothing we hold to which these rights could apply; all of your data is already in your possession on your device, and the Export and Delete controls in Section 9 let you exercise full control over it. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App or in applicable law. The effective date at the top will change accordingly, and the current version will always be available at this address. Because the App collects no data, no change can apply retroactively to information we hold.
