A week is 168 hours. You only get it once.

Tessera 168 is a board of 168 squares — one square for every hour of your week. Tap an hour, pick a purpose, and the hour takes that colour. By Sunday the board is a picture of where your week actually went, not where you meant it to go.

The board

  • All 168 hours on one screen, no scrolling to see your week
  • Nine purposes to start with — Sleep, Work, Commute, Food, Exercise, Family, Myself, Life upkeep, and the grey of hours you have not answered for yet
  • Add your own purposes, with your own names and colours

The day

  • Turn the board to a single day and read it as a timeline
  • Write a note on any hour, or say it out loud and let the phone write it down
  • Keep side tasks inside the hour they belong to, and tick them off there
  • Set a sound for an hour that comes back every week

What it adds up to

  • How the week divided, hour by hour, ranked
  • The change against last week at the same point, so a Wednesday is compared to a Wednesday
  • Hours of sleep a night, and the other things a full week can say

Yours to keep

  • Export a copy, or delete everything permanently, from inside the app
  • Share a week as an image only when you choose to

Private by design. No servers at all.

How your week is handled

There is no account and no sign-in, because there is nothing to sign in to. The week you fill in, the notes you write and the purposes you invent live in a single file on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, because we run no server to upload it to, and no analytics or advertising code is in the app.

Questions? We've got answers.

What is Tessera 168?

A week planner turned around. Instead of writing down what you intend to do, you colour in each hour once it has passed — so what you end up with is a record of the week you actually lived.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, no email, no password. Open the app and start filling in hours.

Where is my data kept?

In one file on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. You can export a copy or delete everything permanently from Settings inside the app.

Are voice notes sent anywhere?

No. Speech is transcribed by iOS on the device itself, and the text stays in the same file as the rest of your week.

What happens if I never buy Pro?

You keep the whole 168-hour board, the built-in purposes and written notes, forever, with no adverts and no nagging. Pro adds your own purposes, voice notes, board styles, sharing and alternate icons.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone on iOS 17 and later. The app speaks English and Vietnamese, and follows whichever one your phone is set to.
