Community

Ask riders who just came back, before you hit the passes

Our Facebook group "Ê, Đi Hà Giang Không? · Ha Giang Loop Travel Community" ("Ê, đi Hà Giang không?" is Vietnamese for "Hey, going to Hà Giang?") is where travellers, locals and SupArrive partners ask about routes, share sourced road updates, find riding buddies and post honest reviews, in Vietnamese or English.

Join the Facebook group Follow SupArrive

Not an official warning or rescue channel. In an emergency call 115 (ambulance), 113 (police) or 114 (fire and rescue). The SupArrive SOS screen shows your coordinates to read out to the operator.

Group rules

Ten rules. When you ask to join, you tick that you agree to them.

  1. Be respectful. No insults, and no discrimination by region, ethnicity, nationality or gender.
  2. Safety first. Posts that show off or encourage drink-riding, riding without a helmet or licence, racing or reckless overtaking will be removed.
  3. Source your road and weather updates. Give the time and place, with a real photo. Warnings need a link to an official source. Unverified posts are labelled or removed.
  4. No accident rumours. Don't post photos of victims or guess at causes. Message an admin if you want to help.
  5. Promotions only in the Friday thread. One comment per business per week. Promotions outside it are deleted, and repeat offenders are banned.
  6. Honest reviews. Share real experiences, with dates. No fake reviews or seeding. If you received a gift, say so. Businesses may reply once, politely.
  7. No doxxing. Don't post other people's phone numbers, IDs, number plates or faces without consent. Send disputes to the admins with evidence.
  8. No restricted sales. No alcohol, drugs or wildlife, no currency exchange, and no renting bikes to people without a valid licence.
  9. Meet safely. Look for riding buddies in the weekly Buddy thread. Report suspicious private messages such as sales pitches, loan requests or scams.
  10. Vietnamese or English. Anything that breaks Vietnamese law or Meta's Community Standards will be removed without notice.

How we moderate

Membership

Answer three short questions and agree to the rules. We may decline brand-new accounts, or requests with no answers, to keep spam out.

Posts

Posts from new members and posts with links are reviewed, usually within 4 hours between 07:00 and 22:00 Vietnam time. Reports are handled within 24 hours.

Fair to everyone

SupArrive partners follow the same rules as everyone else and are never moderators. Genuine complaints about partners are not deleted to protect them.

Enforcement

A private reminder first, then a 7-day posting mute, then a ban. Serious violations such as scams, threats or doxxing lead to an immediate ban.
