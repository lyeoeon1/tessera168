What's inside

🎫

Signed QR card

The code changes every 30 seconds, so a screenshot can't stand in for it. Partners can scan it with no signal. If their camera can't read it, you read out your card number and a 6-digit code for them to type in (this needs signal on the partner's phone).

🗺️

Map and trips

A map of partner businesses on the Loop with each one's perks. Plan the stops for your trip and keep them on your phone.

⛅

Real weather

7-day forecasts for 6 points on the Loop, with heavy rain, wind and fog warnings. The last forecast is kept for offline use.

🆘

SOS

Large-type coordinates to read to the operator, one-tap calls to 115, 113, 114, and a ready-made text with your location for family.

Safety information is always free for everyone. The app is available in Vietnamese and English.

Ride legally, ride sober

In Vietnam you may only ride with a valid licence for the vehicle class: a Vietnamese licence, or an International Driving Permit under the 1968 Vienna Convention together with your home licence. Permits issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention (for example from the US or Australia) are generally not recognised. Travel insurance usually won't pay out if you ride without a valid licence. If you don't have one, go with a licensed easy rider.
