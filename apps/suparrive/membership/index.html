1. What SupArrive is

SupArrive, operated by SupArrive, is a travel member card and information tool for the Hà Giang Loop. It includes a QR card for perks at partner businesses, a partner map, trip planning, weather, and an SOS helper. SupArrive sells Member plans in the app and through partners' gift codes. SupArrive does not sell tours, take bookings or collect payment for partners' services. You pay partners directly.

3. Your card

  • Download SupArrive and enter your name on the welcome screen. The first time your phone has signal, SupArrive's server issues your card number, in the form SA-1234-5678.
  • Each card belongs to one person and one phone. Its QR code is a ticket signed by our server and signed again by your phone's own key every 30 seconds. A screenshot stops working within a minute or two, and copying the card to another phone or editing the app's files doesn't work.
  • Partners check the QR code with no signal. Your phone just needs to go online at least once every 30 days to refresh its ticket. Otherwise partners are told "The card needs refreshing".
  • Your name is in the QR code only if you agree. The app asks once on the Card tab, and the default is no. If you say no, partners see only your card number, and the perks are the same. You can change this in Account → Account & data. Your phone number is never in the code. What stays on your phone and what is on our server is set out in the privacy policy.

4. Buying a Member plan

  • Member plans are bought in the app: open the Card tab → ••• → Member plans, choose 3 days, 7 days, 1 month or lifetime, and scan the VietQR code with your banking app. You pay the price listed in the app. The money goes straight to SupArrive's bank account and is confirmed automatically through SePay. Your card becomes Member as soon as the bank confirms the payment.
  • Member time counts from activation and follows on from any Member time you already have. A lifetime plan never expires. Plans do not renew automatically.
  • If your money arrives after the order's 15-minute window, the plan still activates, usually within a day. Underpaid, overpaid or mismatched transfers are handled by SupArrive staff: a refund, or matching the payment to your order by hand. Each order has a 9-digit order code, shown in the transfer description as SA123456789. Keep it.
  • VAT invoice: after paying, tap "Need a VAT invoice?" and enter the buyer or company name, tax code, email and address. SupArrive's accountant issues the e-invoice and emails it to you.
  • A Member plan belongs to the card it was bought for. It is not transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash. Moving it to a new card when you change phones is covered in section 7.

Partners' gift codes

  • Many partner businesses give or sell Member gift codes: 12 characters, shown as XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. A code sold by a partner must be at the price listed in the app. If anyone asks for a different price, please tell us.
  • Enter the code in the Card tab → ••• → Enter gift code. Entering a code needs signal. Your Member time starts when you enter the code, not when you receive it.
  • Codes must be entered within 14 days of being created. If a code has expired unused, ask whoever gave it to you to swap it for a new one.
  • Each code works once, for one card, anywhere. A code entered while you are already Member adds its time. Gift codes are worth the same as bought ones.

5. Using perks

  1. Open your QR code and let the partner scan it before you pay.
  2. If their camera can't read it, read out your card number and the 6 digits under the code for them to type in. The 6 digits change every 30 seconds. Typing them in needs signal on the partner's phone; scanning the QR code doesn't.

Each perk is the partner's own promotion, applied to the partner's listed prices. Partners commit to honour the perk shown in the app and must give 14 days' notice before they change it. Conditions such as group size, once per day, or blackout dates are always shown on the partner's page.

A partner may decline a perk only in these cases:

  • the code is invalid, expired, a replayed screenshot, or the card needs refreshing because your phone hasn't been online for over 30 days;
  • the perk is not for your tier;
  • you have already used a once-a-day perk today;
  • it is a blackout date shown in the app, or there is force majeure such as floods, landslides or a closed road;
  • you are behaving dangerously or breaking the house rules.

For anything else, report it in the app (Account → Support) and we will follow up with the partner.

6. Refunds and extensions

  • Gift code bought but not yet entered: refundable within 7 days of purchase, where you bought it.
  • Gift code that won't go in because the code is faulty: replaced or refunded, your choice.
  • Underpaid, overpaid or mismatched transfer: SupArrive staff refund it or match it to your order by hand (see section 4).
  • Activated Member plan: not refundable for change of mind. If a SupArrive technical fault stops you using your card, we extend your Member time by the days lost. If it can't be fixed, we refund the unused portion.
  • Force majeure, such as a road closure or natural disaster, forcing you to cancel after activation: contact us within 7 days and we will credit the unused days to a future trip within 12 months.
  • SupArrive makes refunds by bank transfer once a request is approved. The Member time from a refunded order is withdrawn from the card.

7. New or lost phone

  • Account (optional): sign in with Apple under Account. SupArrive receives no name or email from Apple. Plans bought or gifted while you are signed in belong to the account, so they follow you to a new phone with no order code needed. One account works on 2 phones at once; when a third phone signs in, the one used least recently leaves the account. Your name, saved partners, followed communes and liked places sync between phones. SOS contacts, your photo and trips stay on each phone.
  • Leaving or deleting the account never loses a paid plan: if you delete the account, the plan stays on the card of the phone you are using.
  • Without an account: on the new phone, open the Card tab → ••• → Restore a purchase, and enter the order code (9 digits, in the transfer description as SA123456789) and your old card number. The plan moves to the new card, and the old card is no longer Member. Each order can be moved up to 3 times.
  • If you set up the new phone from an iCloud backup, your bought plans move across automatically.
  • Member time from a gift code on a lost phone without an account: contact SupArrive and we can move it to your new card.

8. Weather and safety information

  • Weather forecasts and alerts are for reference only. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo for fixed points on the Loop, and conditions on the passes change very quickly.
  • SupArrive is not an official disaster warning service. Follow instructions from the authorities and people on the ground.
  • The SOS button helps you call national emergency numbers and text your location to family. SupArrive is not a rescue service and can't guarantee phone signal everywhere.
  • You are responsible for how you ride, including holding a valid licence and wearing a helmet.

9. Who is responsible for what

Partners are independent businesses. They are responsible for the quality, price and safety of their own services. SupArrive selects partners against published standards, handles complaints, and acts against partners who break them. SupArrive is responsible for the app, Member plans and gift codes, and the information we write. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under Vietnam's Law on Consumer Rights Protection.

10. Fair use

Don't copy, forge, move to another phone or share QR codes or gift codes so that someone else gets your perks. Don't resell gift codes without our permission, and don't reverse-engineer the app. We may cancel codes that were created or used fraudulently.

11. Complaints

  1. Contact us in the app (Account → Support), by email, with your card number, the partner, the time, and photos or receipts if you have them.
  2. We acknowledge within 2 working days and reply within 10 working days, or up to 30 days for complex cases, with an explanation.
  3. If you're not satisfied, you may seek mediation, complain to Vietnam's consumer protection authority, or go to a competent court. We never force arbitration on consumers.

12. Changes and governing law

We announce changes in the app and on this website at least 15 days before they apply. Changes never reduce the benefits of a Member plan you have already activated. These terms are governed by Vietnamese law.
