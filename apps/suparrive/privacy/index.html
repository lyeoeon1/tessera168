In short: SupArrive keeps on its server only what your card and Member plan need: your card number, Member time, orders, gift codes used and each time a partner applied a perk. Your phone number, photo, SOS contacts and trips stay on your phone. Your location is never sent to us. We don't sell data, show ads or use trackers. You can download or erase your data right in the app, under Account → Account & data. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is SupArrive. Privacy contact: ioniclabs.support@gmail.com.

This policy covers the SupArrive app (for travellers and in partner mode), this website, the partner application form, our contact channels (email, Zalo, phone), and the groups and social media pages SupArrive runs.

Legal basis: carrying out our transaction with you (card, Member plans, invoices), our accounting obligations under the law, and your consent (the account).

How long we keep it

  • Guests' names in partners' ledgers are removed after 12 months.
  • Orders and payment records are kept as long as accounting law requires.
  • Nightly server backups are kept for 30 days.

When a partner scans your card

The QR code holds your card number, tier, Member end date and an anti-forgery signature. Your name and phone number are not in it, so the partner sees only your card number. The partner records the perk in the ledger in their app, and the ledger is sent to SupArrive for reconciliation. Under their agreement with us, partners may use this only to apply perks. They may not use it for marketing or share it.

Network requests

  • Card and Member plans: the app talks to SupArrive's server to get your card number, refresh your card's ticket, buy plans, enter gift codes and sync your account. These requests never contain your location.
  • Payment: you pay by bank transfer in your own banking app, using a VietQR code, straight into SupArrive's bank account. SePay, the service that watches that account's transactions, sends SupArrive the transaction details so we can activate the plan. We never receive your banking login.
  • Weather: forecasts come from Open-Meteo (Switzerland) for fixed points on the Loop. The request contains no location or personal data.
  • Content: partner news and events come from SupArrive's content server. The request contains no personal data.
  • Hearts and follows: counted against a random ID for your phone, not linked to your card or name. A phone that hasn't used the app for 180 days is no longer counted.
  • TikTok videos: videos on the Reels page play in TikTok's embedded player. When you browse Reels, the app asks TikTok for each video's cover and author name (covers stay on the phone for a day at most); when you open a video, TikTok receives the request from your phone and may keep cookies (in a separate area of the app) under TikTok's policy. SupArrive never downloads or stores the videos. Erasing your account clears that area too.
  • Facebook group: the join button opens Facebook; SupArrive reads none of your Facebook data.
  • Maps: maps and directions use Apple Maps under Apple's policy.

The app has no ads, no analytics SDKs, and doesn't track you across other apps. SupArrive's App Store privacy label says data is collected, for app functionality only and never for tracking.

3. This website

No cookies, analytics or ads. Our host (currently GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., USA) may keep technical logs such as IP address and browser type to run and secure the service, under its own policy.

4. When you contact us or apply as a partner

  • Email, Zalo, phone: your name, contact details and message, used to reply, support you with Member plans and handle complaints. Kept for 24 months after our last contact. Complaints are kept for 5 years.
  • Partner application (Google Forms): business name, type, address, contact person, phone, email. Used to consider and conclude a partner agreement. Deleted after 12 months if no agreement is signed.
  • Signed partners: representative and contact details, business documents, and bank details if selling Member gift codes. Kept for the partnership and 5 years after it ends. Accounting records are kept for the period the law requires.

Please don't send ID cards, passports or health information. We don't need them.

5. Facebook group and page

Our Facebook group and page run on Meta's platform, and Meta processes your posts and profile under its policy. Admins use your answers to the joining questions only to approve members. If you choose to leave an email for a guide, we use it only for that, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Moderation logs are kept for 12 months. We only quote your posts elsewhere with your permission or after removing anything that identifies you.

6. Sharing and transfers abroad

We never sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers we need (Supabase for the database, Cloudflare for servers and network, SePay for confirming bank transfers, Apple for Sign in with Apple, Google (including Sign in with Google), Meta (including Facebook Login), Zalo (phone verification codes), our web host, and accounting and e-invoicing services), with partners who scan your card (card number, tier, Member end date), with the partner concerned when we handle a complaint, and with authorities when the law requires it. Card, Member and order data is held on Supabase in Singapore and on Cloudflare. Google, Meta and our web host may also store data outside Vietnam, for example in the US or Singapore. We follow the cross-border transfer procedures that apply to an organisation of our size.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, restrict or object to processing, and complain or seek compensation as the law provides.

  • Right in the app, under Account → Account & data, you can:
    • download a JSON file of everything SupArrive's server holds about your card;
    • delete your account and all your data, with one button. Your card stops working, your name is removed from every partner's ledger (including the copies on partners' phones), your account (if any) is deleted along with its phone number, password and Apple, Google or Facebook link and every phone is signed out, and the data on your phone is erased too. Any Member time left, paid plans included, ends; the app asks you to confirm first. SupArrive keeps only orders (card number and amount) and any VAT invoice request you sent, as accounting records.
  • Data only on your phone: edit it in the app. Uninstalling the app erases only what the app keeps on your phone; your card and Member time stay on the server.
  • Other requests, or data you sent us through our contact channels: write to ioniclabs.support@gmail.com with your card number or details that identify you. We acknowledge within 2 working days and act within the legal deadline, usually within 15 days.

8. Security and breaches

Your QR code is a ticket signed by our server and signed again every 30 seconds by your phone's own key, kept in its Secure Enclave, and it carries minimal data. Only the SupArrive staff who need it can reach the data on our server, each with their own code, and every action is logged under their name. Partner files are kept in accounts protected by two-factor authentication, and only the staff who need them have access. If a breach happens, we notify the authority and the people affected within the legal deadlines.

9. Children

SupArrive is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16 without a parent's or guardian's consent.

10. Changes

We post new versions here and announce them in the app before they apply. If we want to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent again.
