A week is 168 hours. You only get it once.

Tessera 168 is a board of 168 squares — one square for every hour of your week. Tap an hour, pick a purpose, and the hour takes that colour. By Sunday the board is a picture of where your week actually went, not where you meant it to go.

It plays like a puzzle and it reads like a mirror.

Get in touch See the app

Coming to the App Store. iPhone, iOS 17 and later. English and Vietnamese.

What it does

The board

  • All 168 hours on one screen, no scrolling to see your week
  • Nine purposes to start with — Sleep, Work, Commute, Food, Exercise, Family, Myself, Life upkeep, and the grey of hours you have not answered for yet
  • Add your own purposes, with your own names and colours
  • Grey squares are the point: they are the hours you cannot account for

The day

  • Turn the board to a single day and read it as a timeline
  • Write a note on any hour, or say it out loud and let the phone write it down
  • Keep side tasks inside the hour they belong to, and tick them off there
  • Set a sound for an hour that comes back every week

What it adds up to

  • How the week divided, hour by hour, ranked
  • The change against last week at the same point, so a Wednesday is compared to a Wednesday
  • Hours of sleep a night, and the other things a full week can say

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising
  • Your week is one file on your iPhone
  • Voice notes are transcribed on the device itself
  • Export a copy or delete everything, permanently, from inside the app
